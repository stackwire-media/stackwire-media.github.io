Editorial standards and corrections

This page explains how Stackwire prepares, reviews and maintains its articles.

Topic selection and usefulness

We aim to publish practical, clearly explained material about finance and fintech, crypto and web3, and SaaS and AI. Articles should answer a real reader question, explain relevant limitations and add useful context rather than repeat generic summaries.

Sources and factual claims

Writers should support material factual claims with relevant, identifiable sources. For changing topics such as regulations, product features and pricing, we aim to link to primary sources and make the date or assumptions behind examples clear. Readers should check current provider terms and official rules before making decisions.

Review and authorship

Articles are reviewed by an editor before publication. Staff-produced articles may be credited to Stackwire Editorial. Guest contributions should display the contributor's real name and a short, accurate biography. We do not invent author identities, qualifications or professional experience.

AI-assisted work

AI tools may be used to support research, drafting or editing. They do not replace source checking, editorial review or responsibility for the final article. Contributors must verify factual claims and must not submit fabricated quotations, sources, data or credentials.

Commercial relationships and links

Paid or sponsored content should be clearly identified. Links are included when they are relevant to the reader. We do not promise search rankings, guaranteed traffic or a particular link attribute, and we may remove links or reject content that is primarily promotional.

Corrections and updates

If you believe an article contains an error or outdated information, email richartc90@gmail.com with the article URL and the evidence supporting the correction. We review reports and update material errors when verified. Substantive updates should be reflected in the article's updated date where applicable.

Contact

For editorial questions, use our contact page. For contributor submissions, see Write for Stackwire.
