About Stackwire

Stackwire publishes practical articles on finance and fintech, crypto and web3, and SaaS and AI for founders, investors and operators.

How we work

Every article is reviewed by an editor before it goes live. We link to our sources so readers can check them, and we correct mistakes when we find them.

Guest posts and sponsored content

Experts can contribute articles. We publish only work that meets our standards, and we turn down content that promises returns, promotes anonymous projects or reads like an advert. If content is ever paid or sponsored, we label it clearly. Read the guidelines before you submit.
