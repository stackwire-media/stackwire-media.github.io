Write for Stackwire

We publish original articles from people who work in finance, crypto and software. An editor reviews every submission, and we publish only work that meets our standards.

What we publish

  • Original, unpublished articles
  • Finance and fintech, crypto and web3, SaaS and AI
  • Claims backed by named sources
  • Disclose any material use of AI tools and verify all facts, quotations and references

What we reject

  • Copied or generic filler
  • Guaranteed returns or "buy now" language
  • Anonymous projects, scams and meme coin promotions
  • Casino, adult, loan and pharma content

Word Count and Format

  • Recommended length: 800–1,500 words
  • Include an introduction, body sections and a conclusion
  • Include a relevant cover image

Backlink Policy

  • Maximum of 1–2 contextual backlinks
  • Links must be relevant and useful to the reader

How it works

  1. Email your pitch or draft using the address in the submission section below.
  2. An editor reviews your submission and replies within 24 hours.
  3. If accepted, we aim to publish it within 24 hours.

Frequently asked questions

What is a guest post on Stackwire?

A guest post is an original article written by an expert and published on Stackwire under the author’s name. An editor reviews every submission before anything goes live.

Which topics do you accept?

Finance and fintech, crypto and web3, and SaaS and AI. We do not accept casino, adult, loan or pharma content.

Does it cost anything to publish?

Editorial guest posts are free.

How long until I hear back?

We do not guarantee publication. If we are interested in your pitch, an editor may reply by email.

Can I link to my website?

Yes. You may include one relevant link in your author bio, and links inside the article where they genuinely help the reader. We reject articles stuffed with promotional links.

Do you accept crypto promotions?

Only from projects with a real product and a named team that make no promise of returns. We reject anonymous projects, meme coins and anything that guarantees profit.
