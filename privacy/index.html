What we collect

When you email Stackwire, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, email address, website, article draft or message.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to you and to review article submissions. We do not sell it.

Third parties

Cookies and analytics

Stackwire uses Google Tag Manager to manage measurement and analytics tags on the website. The cookies and data collected depend on which tags are configured in the container. Those tags may collect information such as pages visited, device and browser details, approximate location derived from IP address, and interactions with the site. Google services may process this information under Google's own privacy terms. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. We will update this policy if the analytics tools or data uses change.
