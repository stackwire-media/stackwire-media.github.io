Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information Stackwire may receive when you use this website, contact our editors or interact with services embedded in our pages.

Information you provide

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, website, article draft, attachments and message. We use it to respond, review submissions, consider partnership enquiries and handle correction requests.

Website analytics and cookies

Stackwire uses Google Tag Manager to manage measurement tags on the website. The tags configured in that container may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from an IP address, device and browser details, referral information and interactions with the site. The exact data and cookies depend on which tags are active. Google services may process information under Google's own terms and privacy documentation.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to remember settings, measure site use or support services loaded on a page. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Browser controls may not replace a consent choice where applicable law requires one.

Advertising and advertising partners

If Stackwire enables third-party display advertising, the advertising provider and its partners may use cookies, pixels or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise ads and help prevent invalid activity. Depending on the provider and your choices, this may involve information such as your IP address, device identifiers, browser details, pages visited and ad interactions.

Stackwire displays a Native Banner advertisement supplied by Adsterra (AD MARKET LIMITED and/or its advertising partners). The ad is currently placed on the homepage. When you choose “Accept advertising” in the cookie notice, the Adsterra ad tag is loaded from bauval.org and the advertising content is requested. Adsterra and its partners may process your IP address, browser and device information, page URL, referral information, ad impressions and interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, measurement and, where enabled, personalisation. The exact cookies, partners and retention periods may vary by the ad and browser.

The ad tag is not loaded when you choose “Reject”. You can change your choice using the “Cookie settings” control on the homepage. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data or using another browser or device may reset the choice. For more information, read Adsterra's Privacy Policy and its Cookies Policy. This simple site-level choice is not a substitute for any additional consent-management requirements that may apply in your location.

Third-party services and links

Pages may load third-party services, including Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their resources. Links to external websites are governed by those websites' own privacy practices. Stackwire does not control their policies or security.

How we share information

We do not sell the personal information you send directly to Stackwire by email. We may share information with service providers where needed to operate the website, manage measurement, respond to you or meet legal obligations. Third-party services process information under their own terms and policies.

Retention and your choices

We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to handle the enquiry, maintain editorial records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete information you sent, subject to any legal or operational reasons we may need to retain it.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when website tools, advertising partners or data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Before enabling an advertising provider, we will update this page with the provider-specific details and applicable preference controls.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Stackwire editorial team at richartc90@gmail.com or use the contact page.
